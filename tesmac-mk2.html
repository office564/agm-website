TESMAC MK2

Precision Testing. Reliable Results.

Tesmac MK2 is a computerized material testing machine engineered for precision force measurement and consistent testing performance.

High Precision

High-accuracy load cell for precise force measurement and reliable results.

Rugged & Reliable

Sturdy construction designed for consistent and dependable testing performance.

Easy to Use

Intuitive touch-screen interface for simple operation and real-time monitoring.

Versatile

Suitable for a wide range of material testing applications.

TESTING CAPABILITIES

Built for Accurate Performance

Tesmac MK2 provides controlled and repeatable testing for different material and product evaluation requirements.

  • Tensile Testing
  • Compression Testing
  • Peel Testing
  • Bending Testing
  • Quality Control & R&D

TECHNICAL DATA

Tesmac MK2 Specifications

Capacity 20 kgf (196 N)
Travel 300 mm
Accuracy ± 0.5% of Reading
Resolution 0.01 kgf
Speed Range 1–200 mm/min (Programmable)
Load Measurement Load Cell
Display 4.3" Color Touch Screen
Units kgf, N, lbf (Selectable)
Power Supply AC 220V ± 10%, 50/60 Hz
Dimensions (W × D × H) 300 × 400 × 900 mm (Approx.)
Weight 40 kg (Approx.)
