Packaging Testing Solutions

Packaging materials must provide strength, durability, flexibility and reliable performance throughout their intended application.

AGMPL provides testing solutions for evaluating the mechanical and functional properties of packaging materials and components.

Typical Testing Applications

  • Tensile Strength Testing
  • Peel Strength Testing
  • Seal Strength Testing
  • Compression Testing
  • Adhesive Tape Testing
  • Film and Flexible Packaging Testing
  • Paper & Paperboard Testing
  • Label Testing

Why Testing Matters

Packaging testing helps manufacturers maintain product quality, verify material performance and ensure consistent production standards.
