About AG Measurematics

OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

Engineering Precision Since 1993

AG Measurematics was born at IIT Roorkee and built in the heart of India's engineering belt. For over 30 years, we've pushed the boundaries of how industries test, measure, and validate the products the world relies on.

Every system we build is engineered to endure. Purpose-built. Application-specific. Trusted by OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, R&D labs, and certification bodies across India and beyond.

Our systems help manufacturers improve reliability, safety and performance before their products reach the market.

A Legacy of Resilience

Our commitment to precision is matched by our response to global challenges. When the pandemic hit, we put our industrial projects on hold to serve a more urgent need.

Explore our COVID-19 initiative → How we engineered and deployed thousands of critical solutions during the lockdown.

OUR PEOPLE

Engineering for Good

Our COVID-19 initiative

When the pandemic hit, the global supply chain for essential hygiene products collapsed. We realised our expertise in precision engineering could be repurposed to solve a critical problem — the need for reliable, touchless sanitisation.

From Precision to Public Safety

Three core engineering principles guided our effort.

Zero-Contact Reliability Using high-precision infrared sensors to ensure reliable operation.
Industrial Durability Designing units to withstand demanding environments.
Rapid Deployment Streamlining assembly processes for fast delivery.

A Team Without Borders

Job titles were set aside. Everyone had one goal: public safety.

Crossing Disciplines Software and electronics engineers joined the fabrication zone.
Innovative Design Engineers simplified complex industrial technology.
Frontline Commitment Our mechanical team delivered and installed systems in high-risk areas.
